# PetFolio Design System

## App Information
- **App Name:** PetFolio
- **Theme/Vibe:** Modern, elegant, clean, pet-centric, trustworthy
- **Key Elements:** Paw logo, blue color palette, glassmorphism, soft shadows

## Color Palette
- **Primary:** #2563EB (Vibrant Blue)
- **Primary Container:** #DCE8FF
- **Secondary:** #14B8A6 (Teal)
- **Accent/Pet Warmth:** #FFB020
- **Success:** #22C55E
- **Warning:** #F59E0B
- **Danger:** #EF4444
- **Background Light:** #F7FAFF
- **Surface Light:** #FFFFFF
- **Surface Variant:** #EEF4FF
- **Text Primary:** #0F172A
- **Text Secondary:** #64748B
- **Outline:** #CBD5E1

## Typography
- **Primary Font:** Inter
- **Headings:** Bold, clean, high contrast
- **Body:** Highly readable, medium weight for emphasis

## Layout & Components
- **Borders:** "No-line" rule. Use tonal layering and subtle shadows to separate sections instead of 1px solid borders.
- **Corners:** Rounded corners (e.g., 16px to 24px for cards) for a friendly, modern look.
- **Shadows:** Soft, diffused multi-layered shadows to provide depth.
- **Glassmorphism:** Use translucent backgrounds with background blur for floating elements like bottom navigation bars, headers, and modals.
- **Buttons:** Filled pill-shaped buttons for primary actions. Minimalist icon buttons for secondary actions.

## Logo
- **Mark:** A clean, modern paw print symbol in primary blue (#2563EB) combined with a sturdy, elegant wordmark.

## Animations
- Smooth, fluid transitions.
- Micro-interactions on buttons and cards (e.g., scale down slightly on press).
